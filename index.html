A rate is only one number

A Mortgage Quote Is Not a Closing Plan.

Rate matters. But so do points, lender credits, fees, cash to close, reserve requirements, underwriting rules and the ability to close when your purchase contract says you must.

Two mortgage quotes can show similar rates and produce very different costs, liquidity requirements and approval risks.

Before choosing a loan, understand the complete structure.

You Should Know Who Is Helping You Make the Decision.

I founded Jumbo Loan Experts in 2009 after working in wealth management and seeing how often mortgage decisions were reduced to a single question:

“What’s the rate?”

The rate matters. But on a major purchase, it is only part of the decision.

I help borrowers understand the loan structure, cash requirements, costs, underwriting risks and closing path before they commit.

Sometimes that means identifying a better structure. Sometimes it means identifying a problem before it affects the closing. And sometimes the right answer is to wait.

My job is not to push you toward the largest loan or the fastest answer.

My job is to tell you what I genuinely believe you need to know.
